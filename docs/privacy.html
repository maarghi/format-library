Save to My Formats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Save to My Formats ("the extension") is a tool for the Virio team to save LinkedIn posts into a shared Format Library (a Google Sheet). This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with data.

What the extension collects

The extension only collects data when you explicitly click the "➕ My Formats" button on a LinkedIn post and press Save. At that moment it captures, from the post you chose:

  • The post's text, author name, main image URL, reaction and comment counts, and its LinkedIn link.
  • The details you enter yourself: a format name, funnel stage, labels, and an optional note.
  • The first name you set in the extension, used to route your saves to your own tab.

The extension does not collect your browsing history, passwords, email address, cookies, or any personal information beyond the first name you type. It contains no analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not track your activity across websites.

How the data is used and shared

When you press Save, the captured post and your entries are sent to a Google Apps Script web app owned by the Virio team, which writes them into the team's shared Google Sheet (the Format Library). The data is used solely to build and display that library. It is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, and it is not used for advertising or any purpose unrelated to the library.

What is stored on your device

The extension stores a small amount of settings in Chrome's synced storage: whether you've unlocked the library, your chosen first name, and a cached list of labels. This never leaves your Google/Chrome account except through Chrome's own sync.

Permissions

  • storage — remember your settings (name, unlock state, labels).
  • scripting, tabs — add the Save button to LinkedIn posts and keep it working as you navigate within LinkedIn.
  • Access to linkedin.com — to place the Save button and read the post you choose to save.
  • Access to script.google.com — to send your saved posts to the team's Google Sheet.

Data retention

Saved posts remain in the team's Google Sheet until a team member removes them. Local settings remain until you sign out in the extension or uninstall it.
